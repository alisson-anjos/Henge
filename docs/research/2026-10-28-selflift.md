## Useful components

**TST:** adjusts target-video queries according to frame-level attention imbalance.
Potential application: reduce temporal instability in faces, accessories and textures after character replacement.
It changes attention rather than learning or supplying a person-to-picture assignment.
It does not provide independent identity regions for multiple people.
Author-recommended initial strength is tau 0.2; start with a controlled test, not a production default.

**Progressive sampler:** executes an initial portion of the Euler schedule at reduced spatial resolution,
then lifts the latent and completes the remaining steps at target resolution.
`model_hires` permits a different compatible LoRA stack in the high-resolution stage.
This could be useful for generation/refinement cost, including evaluating Sharp only in the finishing stack.
No Henge speed or quality improvement has been measured.

**Masks:** pins the unmasked area to a clean source anchor at every step, and limits transition correction to
the generated region. This is relevant to preserving unselected people and scenery during masked edits.
A union mask still does not bind separate identities to separate people.

## Integration findings

1. TST assigns `transformer_options['optimized_attention_override']` directly. BFS TARA inspects this hook
and rejects unknown attention overrides in `dense_backend_selection`. Stock stacking is therefore not a supported
combination; compose both operations deliberately and test that both remain active.
2. HengeLatentMask emits a NestedTensor noise mask containing video and audio streams. SelfLift's validator
expects a regular tensor mask and calls tensor operations directly on it. Extract/adapt the video mask and
preserve the intended audio semantics rather than passing Henge's mask object unchanged.
3. Progressive sampling accepts standard Euler with zero churn. Henge's configured sampler/schedule must be checked.
4. High-resolution tiling has no cross-tile attention; it rejects masks and disables TST. It is a poor first
experiment for multi-person identity preservation.
5. The H3 adaptation is experimental. Evidence for the paper's image backbones does not establish H3 quality.

## Suggested evaluation order

- TST on/off on a fixed H3 Guide Latents clip, with the current sampler and no tiling.
- Compose TST with BFS TARA only after validating their attention hook interactions.
- Progressive sampling with the external H3 latent upscaler, no tiling, fixed total steps.
- Masked preservation and optional Sharp finishing stack, measuring time, VRAM, identity and unmarked-region drift.

This addresses temporal quality and sampling efficiency. Correct multi-person assignment still needs explicit
reference descriptions plus per-person conditioning, or isolated masked passes when a shared pass confuses identities.
