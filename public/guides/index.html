More control.
Less guessing in the dark.

A practical guide to preparing references, saying who becomes whom and checking the result in Henge.

Click the screenshots to enlarge them. They were captured with the interface in Portuguese; the labels below are the English ones. To compare a change, keep the seed and change one variable per test.

01 · APPEARANCE

Pick references that tell the people apart

Face, hair, cap, glasses, beard and clothing should be visible. The more alike the references are, the more important it is to describe what sets each one apart.

One identity per character

On each character's card, select the matching reference. If you select several Pictures on the same card, they must show the same identity, for example from different angles.

Avoid using a group picture as the reference of a single person.

02 · WHO BECOMES WHOM

The colour follows the person, not the place on screen

Create one character per identity, pick a colour and mark the right person in the video. Bind their Picture on that same card.

Green → Picture 1Cap with gold details and glasses.
Purple → Picture 2Uncovered hair and glasses.
Cyan → Picture 3Navy cap, no glasses.

If the third character moves to the front, they are still the cyan character. If they turn their back, keep the same identity and preserve the guide's orientation.

How to choose the opacity?

The illustrated tests used 100%: the marked area becomes a solid colour. A lower opacity keeps more of the guide's face and expression, but can also let the original appearance influence the swap. Compare on the same segment; no single value works best in every case.

03 · DIRECTION

With multiple characters, the prompt is extremely important

Write an explicit binding for each person. Include the attributes that must stay: which cap, which glasses, hair, beard and clothing.

Character Swap can work well with several people, but it does not always keep the right binding. In our test, fixing the prompt brought back missing attributes and improved the swap of all three characters.

A good prompt answers

  • Which marked person gets each Picture?
  • Which details tell the references apart?
  • What comes from the guide: pose, body direction, gestures, camera and timing?
  • Which people and parts of the scene must stay untouched?
See a complete example for Motion Transfer TARA

The example avoids fixed positions such as “always on the left”. With Exact text, match the trigger to the model: motion_transfer: for Motion Transfer and body_swap: for Character Swap.

motion_transfer:
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is the man in <Picture 1>: full dark beard and mustache, dark aviator sunglasses, a dark pilot cap with a gold emblem and gold details, and an olive-green flight suit with patches. Replace the GREEN-marked man in <Video 1> with this exact appearance from <Picture 1>, including the cap and sunglasses.
<Subject 2> is the man in <Picture 2>: swept-back dark hair, full dark beard and mustache, dark aviator sunglasses, uncovered head, and an olive-green flight suit with red and blue chest patches. Replace the PURPLE-marked man in <Video 1> with this exact appearance from <Picture 2>, including his uncovered dark hair and sunglasses.
<Subject 3> is the man in <Picture 3>: a dark navy baseball cap with a light front emblem, full brown beard and mustache, visible eyes without sunglasses, and an olive-green flight suit. Replace the CYAN-marked man in <Video 1> with this exact appearance from <Picture 3>, including his navy cap and visible eyes.

summary:
[Video 1] motion_transfer: GREEN person becomes <Subject 1> from <Picture 1>; PURPLE person becomes <Subject 2> from <Picture 2>; CYAN person becomes <Subject 3> from <Picture 3>. Each reference supplies only its assigned person's identity, facial features, hair, headwear, eyewear and outfit.

detailed_description:
The three assigned people follow their own source performance frame by frame: the same location, seated body pose, hand gestures, head movement, gaze and facial expression as their corresponding marked person in <Video 1>. Transfer appearance from the assigned picture while keeping the guide's choreography and timing. The green, purple and cyan overlays are temporary selection annotations and disappear completely in the finished video. Keep each assigned identity stable throughout the shot. Preserve the appearance and performance of every unmarked person. Preserve the original classroom, desks, papers, framing, lighting and camera movement.

Auto Prompt / Improve: review the final description of each reference before approving. Exact text: check the consistency of the bindings across the video yourself.

04 · RECIPE

For colour marks, start with TARA

We recommend Motion Transfer TARA with the TARA method and an explicit prompt per character. That combination worked well in the first shot of the illustrated test.

Selecting the TARA LoRA turns the TARA method on automatically. Other LoRAs stay available, with a recommendation notice.

Character Swap can work too. The comparison improved a lot after we described the references more precisely. The recipe still needs to be validated on the other shots before it can be trusted for the whole video.

See the advanced controls

Change one option per test and save the settings with the seed. Stronger parameters do not automatically mean a better identity.

05 · CONTROLLED COMPARISON

Keep the seed or draw a new one

The seed is visible in the settings. Type a number to repeat it, or use Randomize seed. The button only changes the value; it does not start a generation.

To compare a prompt change, keep the seed and the other settings. Once you pick a recipe, try other seeds to check its stability. Changing the prompt, the model or the backend can also change the result.

The base seed is saved with the job and shows when you open the result. You can bring it back together with that test's settings.

06 · CONTINUITY

Review the cuts and mark who reappears

Use camera cuts to separate shots. Context between segments helps continue a take; a camera cut has to be treated as a new shot.

A person seen from behind is still the same person. Add a point in the new shot when they reappear and check the mask. Do not swap their identity just because their position or order on screen changed.

With prompt improvement on, Henge checks who was tracked in each shot and prepares that segment's references. A missing or wrong mark still needs review.

Joins now switch at a point inside the overlap, without blending the pixels of the two segments. That avoids the blur a crossfade creates; pose differences can still cause a jump. A single shot can be redone with an adjusted prompt from its ↻ button.

07 · EDITED AREA

The mask controls where; the prompt helps define who

MASK mode limits the regenerated region and can help preserve the scene and unmarked people. The combined mask alone does not guarantee which reference belongs to each character.

For a hard swap, one approach to try is editing one person at a time, with an individual mask and reference. That flow still needs validation; it is not presented here as a guarantee of a perfect result.

08 · BEFORE THE RENDER

Review the plan and the mask preview

Keep Auto-approve plan off while you are tuning a recipe. Check that the plan described each reference correctly and that each mask is on the right person.

09 · VALIDATE

Compare guide, result and references

Use Guide + result + references to judge the binding and the motion. Pause at the same instant and check every person, including the unmarked ones.

Identity

Do face, hair, cap, glasses, beard and clothing match the right Picture?

Performance

Do pose, gesture, back view and gaze direction stay consistent with the guide?

Preservation

Were the unmarked people and the scene kept?

Continuity

Do identity, accessories or pose change at the joins?

10 · OTHER MODES

Head Swap and Sharpness have different goals

Head Swap

Use it to replace the head/face while keeping the guide's performance. Review the selection and the reference instead of trying to describe a full-body swap.

Sharpness

Use it to refine a video or raise the output resolution. More sharpness does not fix a swapped identity; solve the binding first.

OPTIONAL · PREPARING THE SCENE

An edited frame or a new background

The frame control prepares a reference from one frame to guide the video. Check the frame and the chosen interval before using it; it does not replace reviewing the other shots.

To change the setting, use the New background picture. The moving plate prepares the background following the camera's movement; review the edges and the people's occlusions.

The screenshots show the available controls; no frame was edited and no background was generated for this guide.

OUTPUT · SOUND

Choose where the audio comes from

Keep the original audio copies the guide's sound into the result. Generated audio (H3) uses the sound produced by a compatible model.

Audio conditioning sends the guide's sound to the model to drive the performance. That is not the same as just copying the audio into the final file.

Check the requirements shown next to the controls before using audio generation or conditioning.

REPRODUCE AND COMPARE

Open a test from History

Search by project or model and open the generation you want. Check the result, the plan and the seed it used, instead of relying only on the settings currently in the form.

Naming tests after the model and the main change helps compare versions: for example, “TARA · explicit prompt · seed 42”.

11 · OPTIONAL

Molmo helps analyse; you review the marks

In Settings → Molmo2, choose whether to use the model, the checkpoint folder, how long it stays loaded and the image detail.

Off, planning uses sampled frames; manual marks and SAM segmentation stay available. The Molmo-specific tools are unavailable. This setting refers to running Molmo locally.

Suggest with Molmo2 is blocked during a generation. While a suggestion is running, use Cancel to stop it.

12 · HELP WITH DIRECTION

Use the assistant to prepare and review

First ask for an analysis of the video and the references. Then check the marks and the prompt before asking it to generate.

“Describe what sets these three references apart and check that each one is bound to the right character in every shot.”

A good description has to follow the person when they change position. Review the assistant's proposals: it can also leave out details or confuse identities.

FOR YOUR NEXT TEST

One change at a time

  1. Pick a short segment and references that are easy to tell apart.
  2. Mark each identity and check the masks, including after cuts.
  3. Describe each Picture's attributes and its binding to its colour.
  4. Fix the seed and confirm the model, method and opacity.
  5. Generate and compare person by person; note what was missing.
  6. Change one variable and repeat. Then validate on the other shots.

The screenshots record one version of the interface. Controls and results can change in future versions. Always check the seed and plan saved in your own job.
